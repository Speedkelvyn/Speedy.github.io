# Data

Downloaded on 21 October 2026. All files are in `raw/`.

| File | Contents | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `fred_md_2026-09.csv` | FRED-MD, 126 monthly US macro and financial series from 1959; the first row holds the transformation codes | McCracken & Ng, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis |
| `ebp.csv` | Gilchrist–Zakrajšek credit spread, excess bond premium and recession probability, from 1973 | Federal Reserve Board (FEDS Notes) |
| `BAA.csv`, `AAA.csv`, `GS10.csv` | Moody's seasoned Baa and Aaa yields and the 10-year Treasury yield, monthly averages | FRED |
| `BAA10Y.csv`, `AAA10Y.csv`, `DGS10.csv` | The same spreads and 10-year yield at month end | FRED |
| `NFCI.csv`, `ANFCI.csv`, `STLFSI4.csv` | Chicago Fed financial-conditions indexes and the St. Louis Fed stress index (monthly averages) | FRED |
| `VIXCLS.csv` | VIX, monthly average | FRED |
| `T10Y3M.csv`, `T10Y2Y.csv`, `TB3MS.csv`, `FEDFUNDS.csv` | Term spreads and short rates | FRED |
| `DRTSCILM.csv` | Net share of banks tightening standards on C&I loans (quarterly) | FRED, Senior Loan Officer Survey |
| `DRBLACBS.csv`, `DRCLACBS.csv` | Delinquency rates on business and all loans (quarterly) | FRED |
| `USREC.csv` | NBER recession indicator | FRED |

The ICE BofA corporate and high-yield indexes (`BAML*.csv`) are included but unused: FRED now only provides their last three years.
