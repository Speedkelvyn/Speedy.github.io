02The framework

Six steps, in the order an analyst works. Missing debt, cash or yield data stops the model rather than defaulting to zero.

  1. History & earnings quality

    Five years of statements, screened with the Beneish M-score, Sloan accruals and cash conversion. Grade D withholds the call.

  2. Future cash flows

    FCFF for operating companies, excess returns for banks and insurers, dividends for REITs — routed by industry.

  3. Discount rate

    Ke = rf + β × (Rm − rf) with β regressed on two years of daily returns against the S&P 500, then blended into WACC.

  4. Discount

    Ten-year explicit forecast plus a value-driver terminal value, cross-checked against an exit multiple.

  5. Price

    Enterprise value → equity value → divided by diluted shares, with every bridge item sourced.

  6. Verdict

    DCF, Monte Carlo, comps and peer regression blended into a call and an expected alpha over the required return.
