Approach

A six-step valuation framework

Missing debt, cash or yield data stops the model instead of defaulting to zero, so every published number traces back to a source.

  1. History & earnings quality

    Five years of statements screened with the Beneish M-score, Sloan accruals and cash conversion.

  2. Future cash flows

    FCFF for operating companies, excess returns for banks and insurers, dividends for REITs.

  3. Discount rate

    CAPM cost of equity with beta regressed on two years of daily returns, blended into WACC.

  4. Discount

    Ten-year explicit forecast plus terminal value, cross-checked against an exit multiple.

  5. Price

    Enterprise value bridged to equity value and divided by diluted shares.

  6. Verdict

    DCF, Monte Carlo, comparables and regression blended into a call and expected alpha.
